HYPERLIQUID DAY-TRADER MONITOR — READ ONLY

This monitor DOES NOT place trades.
It reads public Hyperliquid data and evaluates the five configured wallets for daily activity.

SECRETS (only if Telegram report is wanted):

No Hyperliquid private key/API secret is required for this read-only monitor.

Important:
- The five wallets are candidate wallets, not guaranteed winners.
- The monitor intentionally filters out low-activity / long-hold behavior.
- It does not copy or execute any trade.
- Do not connect execution until the 24–48 hour observation period is reviewed.
